The Industry Challenge

Financial institutions struggle with the high CapEx (₹15L+) and recurring branch staffing costs needed to serve semi-urban and rural centers. As a result, millions of citizens must commute 20+ km for basic loan collateral deposits, document attestation, and secure physical filings.

The Vectroplex Engineering Deployment

Vectroplex engineers autonomous unstaffed terminals housed in 3mm hardened cold-rolled steel. Integrated with dual-spectrum optical scanners, biometrics, multi-lingual voice guidance, and FIPS 140-3 cryptographic enclaves, citizens authenticate and deposit physical records 24/7 without on-site branch staff.

  • Broadcom BCM2711 + ESP32-S3 dual-core redundant hardware watchdog
